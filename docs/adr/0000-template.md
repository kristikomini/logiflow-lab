- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. Constraints, not preferences: the deadline, the team size,
the thing that broke, the interview this repository is preparing for. Someone reading this in a
year should understand the situation without having lived it.

## Decision

One sentence, in the active voice, saying what was chosen.

Then the reasoning — but only the part that was actually decisive. A list of every advantage is
marketing; the one or two considerations that tipped it are the record.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were genuinely on the table, and **why each was rejected**. An ADR with no
alternatives is a description, not a decision, and it is the section people skip writing and
later need most.

## Consequences

What this costs. Every real decision has a bill, and writing it down is what stops the next
person re-litigating it from scratch when they hit the cost and assume nobody noticed.

Include what would make this worth revisiting.
