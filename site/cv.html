CV e lettera

Chapter 36 tells you what an Italian technical CV needs. This page produces one in that shape — and then checks it against the chapter's own rules, which is the part reading cannot do for you at 23:00 the night before an application closes.

What it checks, and what it cannot

Every warning traces to a paragraph in chapter 36
  • The GDPR line is added to the Italian version automatically. Its absence is noticed, and some applicant systems filter on it.
  • CEFR levels only. “Good English” means nothing; B2 is a claim they can test.
  • Bullets that describe presence — worked on, mi sono occupato di — are flagged. The formula is what you did → with what → what changed.
  • At least one number somewhere. Measured ones only. “4 s to 90 ms” is the difference between a claim and a fact.
  • Length. One page as a junior; cut the oldest thing, not the newest.
⚠ What no checker can tell you

Whether the bullets are true, and whether they are the right three. A CV full of numbers you cannot defend is worse than a vague one — every noun on it is a question the interviewer may now ask, which is the real function of a bullet and also the risk.
