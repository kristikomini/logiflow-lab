Mock exam

Questions drawn at random from across the whole course, against a clock, with no indication of which chapter each one comes from until after you answer. That last detail is the point: knowing a question is “the async one” does half the work for you, and an interviewer will never tell you.

How to use this
  • Once a fortnight, not daily. This is a measurement, not a study method — the review page is where learning happens.
  • Do not look anything up. A score you inflated tells you nothing, and the wrong answers are the entire value: they go straight into your review deck.
  • 80% is the bar for treating a topic as interview-ready. Below 50%, go back to the chapters rather than sitting it again.
