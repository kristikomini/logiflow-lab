Italian & English, side by side

Technical vocabulary in an Italian office is mostly English — fare il merge, il deploy, la pipeline. The words that will catch you out are the ones that are translated (collaudo, presa in carico, gestionale) and the entire vocabulary of contracts and process, which no technical course teaches and which decides the first phone call.

The one word to learn first

Gestionale. A business-management application — ERP-ish, forms over data, decades of accumulated rules. A very large share of .NET work in Modena, Bologna and Reggio Emilia is building, extending or migrating one. If an advert or a recruiter says gestionale, they are describing the job.
