My notes

Everything you have written on a chapter, collected. Notes are made on the chapter pages themselves — select a sentence and press 📌 Note this, or use the pin button in the top bar for a blank one.

🎓 Why write anything down at all

Copying a sentence out of a chapter does almost nothing. Rewriting it in your own words does a great deal — the effect is called elaboration, and it works because paraphrasing forces you to have actually understood the thing rather than recognised it. The quote at the top of a note is there so you can check yourself against the original afterwards; the part you type is the part that teaches you.

The most useful note you can write is not a definition. It is “this is like X, except…”, or “I got this wrong because I assumed…”.
